# 📝 KhaoNiew

This project aims to provide a user-friendly web application for nutritional monitoring in rural areas. Users can log their meals, and the app will analyze their nutrient intake using AI, showing how much more they can eat that day. Healthcare providers can monitor patients' diets, set dietary limits, and receive alerts when a user exceeds nutrient recommendations.

## 📄 Pages
- Login
- Register
- Meal Overview
- Add/Remove Meal
- Review

### 📝 Add/Remove Meal
On this page, patients can enter the date, meal type, meal name, with carbohydrate content to log their meals. Uploading a photo of the meal is optional. Our AI system is going to analyze the meal name and image in order to automatically estimate the carbohydrate value. The estimated value will then be displayed within the input field. Once all of the information completes, patients are able to submit the record for healthcare providers or doctors to review it. \

### 🤝🏻 Review
As a healthcare provider or doctor, you’ll be able to see all the patients connected to your account. When a patient submits a meal, you can click the Review button to check the details — including the meal photo, name, and estimated carbs. Once you've reviewed it, just enter your name to let the patient know their meal has been checked and approved by you.\
